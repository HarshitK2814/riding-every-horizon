## Bottom line

- H1 (GARCH tracks the risk target better than EWMA): **NOT SUPPORTED**.
- H2 (v1 is not an overfit pick: PBO < 0.5 and deflated Sharpe > 0.90): **NOT SUPPORTED**. PBO 0.030 passes; deflated Sharpe 0.081 fails.
- H3 (crisis alpha follows crash clustering): **NOT SUPPORTED** (Newey-West t = 0.80, needs > 2).
- E1 (conformal filter) and E2 (PySR signal), both exploratory: no evidence of value.
- v1 blend, 2001-2017, v1 costs, lag 1: net Sharpe 0.36 [-0.13, 0.86] over 4385 days. The interval includes zero, so v2 cannot claim the strategy has an edge in the choice period.
- v2 added nothing that beats v1. It added a tougher audit, a realistic cost and capacity picture, and an honest record of what did not work.

## What survives

- **Low PBO.** 0.030 over 12870 CSCV combinations of 96 configs: the config chosen in-sample rarely ranks below the median out-of-sample within 2001-2017. Caveat: this says selection is stable inside the family, not that the family has a real edge.
- **CPCV paths.** 28 splits (8 groups, k_test 2, embargo 21d), 7 selection paths, annualised Sharpe 0.67 to 0.72, median 0.68. Selection almost always picks long-horizon configs. These are still 2001-2017 data, i.e. the choice period, so they are not unseen.
- **Costs realism.** The engine at lag 1 reproduces v1 exactly. Lag 2 changes net Sharpe little (0.36 vs 0.36); market impact is what bites (D1 below).
- **Capacity is small and data-limited.** See D1: capacity (Sharpe within 0.10 of the $10m value) is tens of millions of dollars under square-root impact at most, and Yahoo volume is too thin or wrong for several instruments, so the two ADV variants bracket the truth.
- **Roll-gap sensitivity is reported, not hidden.** 969 flagged days; imputing them moves Sharpe by 0.19, below the CI half-width 0.49, so v1 data is kept.

## Limitations

- Yahoo `=F` series are not back-adjusted; Yahoo volume is unreliable for FX and several metals, so impact and capacity are bracketed, not pinned.
- Sharpe confidence intervals on 2001-2017 include zero; there is no demonstrated edge in the choice period.
- 2018-2026 is a seen period. Only the forward test can add unseen evidence.
- The DSR depends on the trial count and the cross-config Sharpe variance; both are judgment calls (stated above).
- No taxes, financing or short-borrow costs, and no slippage beyond the stated cost and impact model.
