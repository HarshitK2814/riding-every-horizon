## What matched and why the rest differs
- **Fixed-quantity reference matches ManifoldBT** (corr 1.00000 on all legs, max |daily diff| 8.4e-05): ManifoldBT sets quantity = w*equity/price at every close where the target CHANGES, then freezes it. P7 weights are constant between rebalances, so the position's weight drifts with price between fills.
- `run_weights` assumes the weight is held exactly every day, so it differs from ManifoldBT by that drift: corr 0.9983..1.0000, max |dSharpe| 0.012.
- `run_contracts` as written re-targets contracts EVERY day to round(W*NAV/notional), so with notional=1 it behaves like `run_weights`, NOT like ManifoldBT. The plan's expectation (contracts vs mbt corr > 0.999) holds on 38/39 legs only; this is a modelling difference, not an engine bug (daily re-targeting is the intended v1-parity behaviour). Flagged: ['NG=F'].
- Costs: qengine charges a trade on day t+lag; ManifoldBT charges at the fill close t, one day earlier. Totals agree within ~1% (cost_w vs cost_c vs reference).

## Gotchas found
- `ExecutionConfig.max_position_pct` defaults to 1.0 and silently caps |weight| at 100% of equity: UST2 (max |w| 4.19) had corr 0.957 until `max_position_pct=10.0`.
- `pyramiding=True` made a leg's equity go negative (accumulates instead of retargeting); left at default False.
- Time window is half-open: ManifoldBT returns 4383 bars vs 4385 weights.

## Limits
No roll cost, no impact, no slippage in the ManifoldBT run; synthetic OHLC (no gaps); positions only change on weight-change days. Sharpe values here carry no CI because they are parity diagnostics, not performance claims.
